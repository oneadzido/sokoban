# Privacy Policy for Sokoban

**Last Updated:** September 6, 2026

---

## Overview

Sokoban is a classic puzzle game for Android that respects your privacy. This document explains how the app handles your data.

---

## No Data Collection

Sokoban does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

**Sokoban does not:**

- Request your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information
- Monitor or track your location
- Incorporate analytics, crash reporting, or advertising tools
- Gather device identifiers or hardware information
- Access your contacts, messages, or call history
- Integrate with any third‑party tracking services

---

## Local‑Only Storage

All information processed by Sokoban remains exclusively on your device. No data is ever transmitted over the internet.

Sokoban stores the following information locally:

- **Game progress** (current level, grid state, player position, move count, timer, completed levels) – stored in `SharedPreferences` as a JSON string
- **Undo history** – kept in memory only; cleared when the app is closed
- **FontAwesome font file** – loaded from assets for icon rendering

---

## Permissions

Sokoban requests **no special permissions** beyond those automatically granted by the Android system. The game operates entirely offline and does not require internet, storage, or location access.

---

## Third‑Party Services

Sokoban integrates **no third‑party analytics, advertising, or tracking services**.

The app uses two open‑source libraries:

- **Gson** – for JSON serialisation of game state
- **FontAwesome** – for vector icon rendering

Both libraries operate entirely locally and do not transmit any data.

---

## Security

All data remains securely on your device. Sokoban does not store, process, or transmit your information elsewhere. Your game progress stays private and under your complete control.

---

## Children's Privacy

Sokoban does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. The app contains no social features, user accounts, or data collection mechanisms.
